# Carbon Robotics - Senior Robotics Software Engineer

## Company
Carbon Robotics

## Location
Seattle, WA (Remote possible)

## About the Role
Carbon Robotics is looking for a Senior Robotics Software Engineer to join our growing team. You will work on cutting-edge computer vision and deep learning systems for autonomous robots.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize computer vision algorithms
- Design and implement deep learning models
- Collaborate with hardware engineers
- Conduct code reviews and mentor junior engineers
- Participate in architecture design decisions

### Technical Skills

- Knowledge of CUDA and GPU programming
- Understanding of real-time systems
- Ability to optimize deep learning models for edge devices

### Education & Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related field
- Master's degree in Machine Learning or Computer Vision preferred
- Required: Understanding of convolutional neural networks

### Soft Skills

- Ability to work in a fast-paced startup environment
- Experience working with cross-functional teams
- Must be able to manage multiple projects simultaneously
